<p>VELVET RUINS now continues as VELVET RUINS 2, a curated 256-preset Vital collection for dark ambient, cinematic, industrial, experimental electronic, horror, game-audio, and soundtrack work.</p>
<p>This article was first published for the original bank in March 2026; the details below describe the current expanded edition.</p>
<p>Version 2.0 adds 176 presets to the original 80, with 55 cinematic experiments, 28 dark basses, 41 dark sequences, 47 drones and pads, 26 experimental drums, 26 ritual keys and plucks, and 33 SFX and textures.</p>
<p>Every preset includes ATMOS, MOTION, DUST, and SPACE macros. The update also improves effect efficiency, low-end mono compatibility, velocity response, modulation routing, macro behavior, and output consistency.</p>
<p>The free VELVET RUINS Lite edition now includes 32 presets selected from the expanded collection.</p>
