<p>The Kreativ Sound catalog now reflects BIOFORMS 2's expanded Synplant 2 bank and its free Lite edition. October 25 is the website-update date, not a claim about the original Gumroad release date.</p>
<p>BIOFORMS 2 costs €9 and contains 128 presets across BASS, DRONE, FX, KEYS, MULTI, PAD, and VOX. Version 2 adds 93 cinematic, abstract, and wild variations alongside the core sounds, with consistent category naming and refined note response across the new material.</p>
<h2>Try 32 sounds free</h2>
<p>BIOFORMS Lite includes 4 basses, 4 drones, 6 FX, 4 keys, 5 multis, 5 pads, and 4 vocal textures. Enter 0 on Gumroad to download free; contributions are optional. All 32 Lite sounds are in Full, so owning both gives you 128 unique presets, not 160.</p>
<h2>Requirements and installation</h2>
<p>Sonic Charge Synplant 2 is required and is not included. Synplant 1 is not supported. Use a compatible macOS or Windows setup; a minimum tested point version is not recorded for Full.</p>
<p>Unzip the pack and load the .synplant files in Synplant 2, or install them in its user preset folder. Both editions include a preset list, installation readme, and license. Read the included license before redistributing anything; commercial use of the sounds does not permit sharing or reselling the preset files.</p>
