<p>OBSIDIAN and OBSIDIAN Lite are now listed on the Kreativ Sound website. This is the catalog-addition date, not a claim about the original Gumroad release date.</p>
<p>The full bank costs €9 and includes 128 native Serum 2 presets: all 32 Lite sounds plus 96 more. Lite is free with optional contributions, not an additional exclusive bank.</p>
<h2>Requirements and installation</h2>
<p>Your own Xfer Serum 2 and a compatible DAW are required. Tested with Serum 2.1.5; earlier versions are unverified. Serum 1 is not supported.</p>
<p>Extract the ZIP, copy the bank to Serum 2's Presets/User folder, and refresh its browser. Both editions include named macros, musical descriptions, an installation guide, a preset list, and a commercial-use license.</p>
