<p>The footer was updated to make the wider Made by KREATIV network easier to find without turning product pages into a link directory. The site now keeps attribution compact and sends visitors to the central network page when they want to explore related projects.</p>

<h2>What changed</h2>
<p>Footer attribution was consolidated into one centered line. Instead of repeating a long list of project links across every page, the site links to Made by KREATIV as the parent destination for related tools, typography, WordPress work, and sound products.</p>

<p>This keeps Kreativ Sound pages focused on the task in front of the visitor: browsing sound packs, opening tools, reading release notes, or finding contact details.</p>

<h2>Why it matters</h2>
<p>Footer links are useful when they provide context, but they can also distract from product decisions. A simpler footer gives the site a cleaner ending while still preserving a path to the wider ecosystem.</p>

<p>The update also makes future maintenance easier. If the network changes, the central Made by KREATIV destination can carry that structure instead of forcing every site footer to be rewritten.</p>
