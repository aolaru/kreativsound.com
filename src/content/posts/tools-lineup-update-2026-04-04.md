<p>The Kreativ Sound tools area is being focused around two practical browser workflows: Preset Mutator for Vital preset generation and Wave Mutator Lite for local sample cleanup.</p>

<h2>Preset Mutator</h2>
<p>From Scratch, Mutate Preset, and Audio to Preset now live inside the same Preset Mutator app instead of being treated as separate public products. This keeps the tool easier to understand: choose an intent, a source preset, or a short audio file, then export playable Vital starting points.</p>

<p>The current version is intentionally narrow. Vital export is live first. Serum 2 and Pigments 7 remain planned future targets, but the public promise is centered on the workflow that works now.</p>

<h2>Wave Mutator Lite</h2>
<p>Wave Mutator Lite covers a different need: preparing messy WAV material for sample packs, preset-source audio, one-shots, loops, and short product previews. It trims silence, adds fades, normalizes peak level, exports cleaned files, and can build a browser-made preview montage.</p>

<p>Both tools stay local-first. Files are processed in the browser, and the tool pages are designed for fast sound-design tasks rather than replacing a full DAW or editor.</p>
