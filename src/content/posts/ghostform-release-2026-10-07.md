<p>Released on September 25, 2026, <a href="/plugins/ghostform">KS Ghostform</a> is a free synthesizer for evolving drones, strings, and slow cinematic movement. Version 1.0.0 needs no activation or license key.</p>

<h2>What is included</h2>
<p>144 factory presets, two oscillator workflows, a sub oscillator, filter movement, echo, reverb, and a Variation engine. The download includes the Ghostform User Manual v1.0 as a PDF.</p>

<h2>Requirements</h2>
<p>On macOS, use an AU- or VST3-compatible host on macOS 11 or later; the universal build supports Intel and Apple Silicon. On Windows, use a 64-bit VST3-compatible host. Ghostform is a plugin, not a standalone application.</p>

<h2>Download and install</h2>
<p>Get the free files on Gumroad, then follow the <a href="/plugins/ghostform#product-installation-title">installation instructions</a> for your platform. Start with a factory preset and hold a note to hear its slow movement.</p>
