<p>ANALOG DRIFT and its free Lite edition are now listed on the Kreativ Sound website. This is the catalog-addition date, not a claim about the original Gumroad release date.</p>
<p>Full costs €9 and contains 256 presets per Mini V edition: 89 canonical designs plus 167 organic or wild variants. Lite selects eight basses, eight sequences, eight pads, and eight effects. All 32 Lite sounds are also included in Full.</p>
<h2>Choose your Mini V edition</h2>
<p>Your own Arturia Mini V2, V3, or V4 is required. Native banks use .minibank, .minix, and .min4x respectively. V4 User presets, installation notes, a preset list, and a product license are included.</p>
<p>V3 was checked through native rendering. V2 playback in a licensed installation remains unverified; V4 loading checks were representative and in demo, with complete audio and level comparisons still pending. Native menu import of the release archives has not been verified. V4 recreations can sound different from V2 and V3.</p>
