<p>BIOFORMS 2 works best when one layer moves and one layer stays still. Start with the free Lite selection or the full 128-preset bank.</p>

<h2>1. Start with one stable base</h2>
<p>Load a restrained BIOFORMS 2 preset in Synplant 2 and hold one note for four bars. Keep this track as the tonal center.</p>

<h2>2. Add one moving layer</h2>
<p>Load a more animated preset on a second Synplant 2 track, playing the same note. Lower its fader, then mute and unmute it: keep it only if the movement is clearer without masking the base.</p>

<h2>3. Leave space around it</h2>
<p>BIOFORMS 2 already has detail. Do not stack too much on top. A small amount of space makes the motion feel more alive.</p>

<p>The main idea is simple: one layer for tone, one layer for motion.</p>
