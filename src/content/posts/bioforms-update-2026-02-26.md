<p>BIOFORMS was updated across the homepage and catalog so Synplant 2 users can understand the pack faster. The product entry now puts the focus on organic motion, evolving tones, and ambient beds instead of leaving the pack buried as a generic preset listing.</p>

<h2>Catalog focus</h2>
<p>The updated placement gives BIOFORMS a clearer role inside the catalog: living movement, soft mutation, and restrained texture. That makes it easier to compare against heavier packs such as BLACK ARCOLOGY or more spectral Vital material such as VELVET RUINS.</p>

<p>The product copy and calls to action now point visitors toward one obvious path: inspect the sound direction, confirm the Synplant 2 requirement, and open the Gumroad listing when the palette fits the session.</p>

<h2>Best use</h2>
<p>BIOFORMS works best when a track or cue needs motion under the surface. Use it for ambient beds, evolving support layers, subtle melodic movement, and organic texture that should stay alive without crowding the mix.</p>
