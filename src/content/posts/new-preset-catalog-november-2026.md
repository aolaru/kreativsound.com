<p>The Kreativ Sound catalog now includes three more preset collections and their free editions.</p>
<p><a href="/sounds/brutus-imposcar-presets">BRUTUS</a> brings atmospheric sounds to GForce impOSCar 2 and 3. <a href="/sounds/brutus-lite-imposcar-presets">BRUTUS Lite</a> offers 16 sounds per instrument. The instrument-specific versions are related and may sound different; check the compatibility notes before downloading.</p>
<p><a href="/sounds/strange-signals-sylenth1-presets">STRANGE SIGNALS</a> brings 122 atmospheric and experimental presets to LennarDigital Sylenth1. Try 16 selected sounds with <a href="/sounds/strange-signals-lite-sylenth1-presets">STRANGE SIGNALS Lite</a>.</p>
<p><a href="/sounds/surgical-cz-surge-xt-presets">SURGICAL CZ</a> is a free bank of 132 CZ-inspired presets for Surge XT, including eight alternate versions.</p>
