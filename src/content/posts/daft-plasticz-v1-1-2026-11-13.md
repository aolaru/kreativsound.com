<p><a href="/sounds/daft-plasticz-presets">DAFT</a>, the free legacy preset collection for reFX PlastiCZ, has been updated to version 1.1.</p>

<p>PlastiCZ is discontinued and is not included. A compatible PlastiCZ installation and host are required.</p>
