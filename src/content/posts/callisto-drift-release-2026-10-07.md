<p><a href="/sounds/callisto-drift-jup-8-v4-presets">Callisto Drift</a> brings cold pads, dark sequences, basses, and expressive leads to Arturia Jup-8 V4. The full bank costs €9; <a href="/sounds/callisto-drift-lite-jup-8-v4-presets">Callisto Drift Lite</a> is free.</p>

<h2>Full or Lite</h2>
<p>The full v1.1 bank includes 128 presets: 47 pads, 43 sequences, 15 basses, 16 leads, 5 FX, 1 key, and 1 choir. A PDF preset catalogue, README, release notes, and license are included.</p>
<p>Lite includes 32 presets: 12 pads, 8 sequences, 4 basses, 4 leads, 2 FX, 1 key, and 1 choir, plus its PDF catalogue. Try it in a short sketch before choosing the full bank.</p>

<h2>Requirements and installation</h2>
<p>Arturia Jup-8 V4 is required and is not included. The banks use native <code>.jup4x</code> files and were tested with version 4.6.4.6366. Download the ZIP, extract it, and import the bank through the instrument's preset browser.</p>
