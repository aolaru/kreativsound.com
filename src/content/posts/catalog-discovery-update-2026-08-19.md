<p>The <a href="/sounds/">Kreativ Sound catalog</a> now starts with direct paths for common synths and sound-design needs, so visitors can narrow the collection before scanning every product.</p>

<h2>Browse from a useful starting point</h2>
<p>Choose Arturia JUN-6 V, Pigments, Vital, FM8, or Softube Models to find compatible preset banks. The same catalog panel also offers direct paths for ambient, cinematic, industrial, FX and transitions, and free packs.</p>

<p>The existing full catalog, product categories, search, and product pages remain available. The change simply makes the first decision faster when you already know the instrument or sound direction you need.</p>
