Wave Mutator Lite beta

Changelog

A public record of release milestones, practical improvements, and the current scope of the local sample-cleanup workflow.

  1. Current beta release

    v0.2.1

    August 21, 2026

    Added delivery profiles for recurring sample-pack, music-pack, game-SFX, and store-preview tasks.

    • Delivery Profiles now set practical cleanup, naming, export, pack, and preview defaults.
    • Preflight and export controls make the selected production target easier to review before rendering.
    • The workflow remains local: source audio is decoded, processed, and exported in the browser.

  2. v0.2.0

    August 20, 2026

    Introduced the public beta for preparing cleaner WAV files, ZIP packs, and short MP3 preview montages.

    • Added silence trimming, short fades, peak normalization, clipping warnings, and browser preview processing.
    • Added single-file WAV, ZIP queue, manifest, and MP3 montage export paths.
Current beta limits

Wave Mutator Lite does not yet offer LUFS normalization, spectral editing, AI tagging, or full DAW-style repair. The tool is designed for clear, local preparation rather than replacement of a mastering or restoration suite.

Wave Mutator Lite is a local-first Kreativ Sound browser tool.
