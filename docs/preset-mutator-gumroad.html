Preset Mutator Pro creates playable preset starting points for Vital and Serum 2 beta. Choose a direction, analyse a short sound, or mutate a preset you already use. Generate 32 variants and download the complete ZIP pack. Your source presets and audio stay in your browser.

What is included

  • 32 preset variants per generation and complete ZIP export
  • From Scratch, Audio to Preset, and Mutate Preset workflows
  • Controls for direction, movement, texture, and variation strength
  • Vital stable export and Serum 2 beta export

Supported synths and requirements

  • A modern web browser
  • Vital for .vital presets, or Serum 2 for .SerumPreset files; the synth itself is not included
  • Serum 2 is a beta target. Treat generated presets as starting points to refine in the synth.
  • Arturia Pigments beta is available in Preset Mutator Free only; it is not included in Pro.

Activate Pro

  1. Copy the license key from your Gumroad purchase or library.
  2. Open Preset Mutator Pro, paste your key into the activation field, and activate.

Gumroad license-key verification needs an internet connection. Your key is saved in this browser. Earlier signed Pro tokens remain supported.

Activation sends your license key to Gumroad for verification, not your source presets or audio. Preset generation stays local.

Missing your key? Find your Gumroad purchase or contact Kreativ Sound.
